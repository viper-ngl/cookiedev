=== DNS RECORDS FOR EMAIL SENDING (MailChannels + Purelymail) ===

Add these records in Cloudflare DNS for parksidestore.cc:

1. Allow MailChannels to send on your behalf (SPF):
   Type:  TXT
   Name:  @
   Value: v=spf1 include:relay.mailchannels.net include:purelymail.com ~all
   (If you already have an SPF record, merge them - only one SPF record allowed)

2. MailChannels Domain Lockdown (prevents spoofing):
   Type:  TXT
   Name:  _mailchannels
   Value: v=mc1 cfid=parksidestore.cc

3. DMARC (tells receivers what to do with failed emails):
   Type:  TXT
   Name:  _dmarc
   Value: v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:noreply@parksidestore.cc

These three records let noreply@parksidestore.cc send emails
through Cloudflare Pages (MailChannels) for free.

=== HOW EMAIL IS USED IN THE SITE ===

- When someone applies for a job → Discord DM confirmation sent instantly
- When application is accepted/rejected → Discord DM notification sent
- The noreply@parksidestore.cc address is shown as the sender

=== PURELYMAIL SMTP (optional, for external email clients) ===

If you want to READ emails sent TO noreply@parksidestore.cc
in an email client (Outlook, etc.):

  Incoming (IMAP):  imap.purelymail.com  port 993  SSL
  Outgoing (SMTP):  smtp.purelymail.com  port 465   SSL
  Username:         noreply@parksidestore.cc
  Password:         (the password you set in Purelymail)
